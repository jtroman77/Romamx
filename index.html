Method, coverage and limits

Markets settle on total goals by halftime, including first-half added time. This version only analyses fixtures not yet started; it does not predict live matches. The default scan covers the next two hours and up to five earliest eligible fixtures. It scans only leagues and history returned by your plan.

History: up to 180 days, latest 30 valid completed matches per team. Missing halftime scores are excluded, never treated as 0–0. Home/away-matching games receive full weight, others 40%; different-league games receive half weight. Recency uses a 60-day half-life. Scoring and conceding averages estimate expected first-half goals; a Poisson count estimate is blended equally with smoothed historical Over/Under frequency. Shared fixtures count once in that frequency.

Qualifiers require 10 results per team, 5 in the relevant home/away role, 5 in the current league, recent history within 45 days, sufficient effective sample, agreement between team profiles and a conservative score of at least 60 after goal-rate sensitivity and historical uncertainty checks. These are unvalidated screening thresholds. Scores are model leans, not calibrated win probabilities or guarantees. This version does not adjust for current lineups, injuries, opponent strength or xG. Check those separately before using a pick.

Requests are capped at 60 per operation, with no automatic retry or background scanning. Completed history is reused in this tab for 30 minutes. A partial/failed scan produces no active recommendation. Results expire after five minutes, and selection rechecks the fixture. If a browser blocks the connection, try serving this HTML on your existing HTTPS site; if it remains blocked, a private backend is needed. Do not embed your token into public code.
